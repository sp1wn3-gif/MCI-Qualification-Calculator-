Green is Copilot. Blue is agents. A green bar is what the Copilot licence position delivers on its own. Blue is what has to come from agent consumption or agent MAU. Attainment blends, so a size that is part green and part blue still passes, provided the blue actually materialises.

Selecting sizes. Pick one size per engagement. Sizes within a single engagement cannot be combined: claiming one body of work at several sizes is duplicative funding under the incentive guide. Different engagements do combine, because each is distinct work with its own scope, deliverables and outcome. A tenant allows two approved claims with only one active at a time, so a multi-engagement programme runs in sequence and you can no longer run all three pots against one tenant in a single year.

On purchase. A size tagged "on purchase" is reachable only once the planned licences land. Fresh licences are paid units with no active users behind them, so the whole block counts as MAU gap on day one. Nominate as they land. Every user who starts working closes the gap and can drop the tenant into a smaller size, or out of the pot entirely.

October 2026 repricing. Envisioning rates were cut at XS, S and M, and its attainment targets fell with them. Pots 02 and 03 keep their headline totals but are now two-stage nomination engagements: 65% is guaranteed on POE approval, and the remaining 35% pays only if the nominated band is reached in the six-month evaluation window. Overshooting pays no more. Missing it pays nothing. Claims nominated before 30 September 2026 keep the old rates.

Nomination and timings, Pots 02 and 03. The band is fixed at nomination and cannot be changed, and a nominated customer cannot be re-nominated by Phoenix or by any other partner for the rest of the programme term. Consent within 30 days of nomination, POE within 90 days of consent, then a six-month evaluation window starting the month after POE submission. Pot 03's Stage 2 is described as gated on Copilot and Agent consumption revenue, so agent MAU alone may not release it; confirm with Microsoft before pricing Stage 2 into a deal.

Source: Microsoft Commercial Partner Incentives Guide, edition 5 October 2026.
